Every line below is plain Bracify markup. Source: book #1 "{sample.title}".

Built-in pipes

date{sample.published_at | date: 'yyyy/mm/dd'}
number{sample.price | number}
currency (default JPY){sample.price | currency}
currency USD{sample.price | currency: 'USD'}
uppercase{sample.meta.publisher | uppercase}
lowercase{sample.meta.publisher | lowercase}
truncate{sample.summary | truncate: 20}
default{noMeta.meta.isbn | default: 'no isbn'}
ternary (true){sample.featured | ternary: 'featured', 'regular'}
ternary (false){noMeta.featured | ternary: 'featured', 'regular'}
chain{sample.summary | truncate: 12 | uppercase}
json
{sample.tags | json}

Arithmetic

  • {sample.price} x 1.1 = {sample.price * 1.1}
  • ({sample.price} + 100) x 3 = {(sample.price + 100) * 3}
  • {sample.pages} % 7 = {sample.pages % 7}
  • division by zero is safe: {sample.pages / 0}
  • piped: {sample.price * 1.1 | number}
  • next offset: {_sys.query._offset + 10}

Conditions

  • featured is truthy
  • draft is not featured
  • status = published
  • status is not draft
  • price > 1000
  • price >= 1500
  • stock < 10
  • stock <= 4
  • price >= 1000 AND stock > 0
  • status is draft OR published
  • compares with a variable
  • never shown
